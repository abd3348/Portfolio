### Projects

#### IT GUIDE

A smart web-based platform designed to help Information Technology students manage their academic journey and plan their future careers.

Main features include:

* GPA calculation and prediction
* Academic progress tracking
* Interactive study plans
* Course information
* Course prerequisite relationships
* Academic planning

**Technologies:** ASP.NET Core MVC, C#, Entity Framework Core, SQL Server, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and AJAX.

#### HEALTH CENTER

A simple health center management system designed to organize appointments between doctors and patients.

The system allows:

* Doctors to create available appointment slots
* Patients to book available appointments
* Preventing already-booked appointments from being selected again
